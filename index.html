Know the Rules Before You Trade

How Long Is the Miami Plan Available?

One month only. You can buy Miami Plan accounts until [end date].

What Is the Soft DLL?

Hitting the daily loss limit flattens your positions and pauses trading until the next session. The account stays open; only the static drawdown ends it. Soft DLL applies in both evaluation and sim funded, and is priced about a third below the No DLL version.

Can I Pass the Evaluation in One Day?

Yes. Hit the profit target once. There are no minimum trading days and no consistency rule.

Which Contracts Can I Trade?

Micros only: up to 5, 10 or 20 micros on 10K, 25K and 50K accounts in evaluation and sim funded, and up to 20 micros on the live account.

How Do Sim Payouts Work?

Payouts are daily, with a minimum gross payout of $200, $400 or $600 for 10K, 25K and 50K accounts. Each payout is split when you request it: 75% to you, 10% to your live fund and 15% to Tradeify. The daily profit cap is $2,000, $4,000 or $6,000.

When Do I Move to a Live Account?

When your payouts plus current profit reach $12,000. At that point your profit is paid out in full and you move live with a $1,500 static balance: your $1,200 live fund plus a $300 Tradeify top-up. The static balance is not withdrawable and is never reduced or removed.

How Do Live Payouts Work?

Daily, with a $250 minimum, on all profit above the $1,500 static balance. The live profit split is 90/10.

What Happens if I Breach the Live Account?

A live breach ends the live account, and you start a new evaluation.

What Happens to an Unfilled Live Fund?

An unfilled live fund rolls into your next account of any size if you buy it within 30 days.

How Many Accounts Can I Hold?

One active account per size, and two in total.
